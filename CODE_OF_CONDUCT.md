# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss technical decisions with evidence and leave room for
different experience levels. Criticism should address the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, and publication of another person's private information are
not acceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when needed to
protect contributors. Enforcement should explain the behavior and the action taken.

Contact the repository maintainer through an available private channel for sensitive concerns.
Do not include private reports in public issues or pull request discussions.
